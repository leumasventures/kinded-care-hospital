About Kinded Care

Vision, mission and core values

Our vision

To be the leading private hospital in Abia State renowned for excellence in maternal, child and family healthcare, where every mother and child receives compassionate, safe and affordable care.

Our mission

  1. To provide high-quality, evidence-based medical and maternity services 24/7.
  2. To ensure safe deliveries and reduce maternal and neonatal mortality through skilled care and emergency preparedness.
  3. To treat every patient with dignity, respect, kindness and confidentiality.
  4. To promote preventive healthcare and health education in our community.
  5. To maintain highest standards of professionalism, hygiene and ethics.

Our core values: C-CARES

  • C

    Compassion

    We care with empathy for mothers, babies and families.

  • C

    Competence

    Skilled, continuous training and up-to-date practice.

  • A

    Accountability

    Responsible for our actions and patient outcomes.

  • R

    Respect

    Dignity for all, regardless of status, tribe or gender.

  • E

    Excellence

    Striving for the best in every procedure and service.

  • S

    Safety

    Strict infection control and safe clinical practices.
